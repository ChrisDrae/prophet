Reading the panel

  • Row 1 makes the tone: LFO, Poly Mod, the two oscillators, drift and the mixer.
  • Row 2 shapes it: both filters and both envelopes. Low-pass cutoff is the one large knob.
  • Row 3 is how it plays and where it goes: voice modes, glide, aftertouch, distortion, the two effects.
  • A small red light is a switch. In LFO, Poly Mod and Aftertouch the lights pick destinations.

Playing this draft

Computer keys A W S E D F T G Y H U J K play from C3. The strip shows 49 keys, like your Code 49. The mod wheel adds vibrato when the LFO's Freq lights are on.

The light behind the tiles follows the sound: oscillator levels warm it, low-pass cutoff brightens the red, high-pass and effects add blue, LFO and Poly Mod add violet and speed.
